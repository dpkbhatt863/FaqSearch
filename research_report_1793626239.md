## 1. Introduction  

Road‑ster motorcycles combine the relaxed, upright riding posture of a cruiser with a lighter, sport‑inspired chassis. For riders with a shorter inseam, the decisive factor is **seat‑height** – the distance from the ground to the centre of the saddle. A low seat enables confident “flat‑footing” (both feet touching the ground while seated), which improves balance at stops, reduces the risk of low‑speed tip‑overs, and generally speeds up the learning curve.

India’s 2026 two‑wheeler market is saturated with commuter‑grade machines (150‑200 cc) and a growing segment of mid‑range “road‑ster” bikes (220‑350 cc). The purpose of this report is to identify, from the currently available Indian‑market models, the **single road‑ster motorcycle that offers the best overall value for a short rider**.  

The analysis draws on the latest editorial lists (Car & Bike, GoDigit, BikeWale) and on publicly available specifications (manufacturer data sheets, dealer price lists).

- Sources are partially truncated; full citation details (access dates, page titles) are missing, which hampers verification.  

**One line verdict:**  
The report makes a compelling case for the Bajaj Avenger 220 as the best‑value road‑ster for short Indian riders, but its impact would be stronger with a broader model set, more rigorous weighting justification, and richer cost‑of‑ownership data.
